chipvoice

Four lines of text, four channels, one real sound chip. Everything below runs on a cycle-accurate 2A03 - the NES chip - in an AudioWorklet. Not oscillators. Press FIRE while the music plays: the shot takes pulse 2 away from the chord, exactly as it did on the hardware.

tap play to start the chip

One token per sixteenth note. A note name (A4, F#3), . to hold, = to cut. Drums use K kick, S snare, H hat, O open hat. The bass line's token count sets the pattern length, so a bar in five is possible.
